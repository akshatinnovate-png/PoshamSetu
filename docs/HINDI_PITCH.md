# PoshanSetu — Hindi pitch (written in English letters)

Speak in Hindi, read in English letters. English meaning of key lines in brackets.
Plan: start with Riya's numbers and the question "kya yeh bachchi theek hai?". Show the app only if a minute is left (Riya's chart).

## 1. The 2-minute pitch

> Namaste. Hum ek sawaal se shuru karte hain.
>
> Yeh Riya hai, do saal ki bachchi, Jharkhand ke ek gaon ki. Har mahine Anganwadi mein uska wazan hota hai:
> February — **9.1 kilo**. March — **9.1**. April — **9.1**. May — **9.1**. June — **9.1**. July — **9.1**.
>
> **Aap bataiye — kya yeh bachchi theek hai?** *(Is this child okay?)*
> *(Pause. Let them answer.)*
>
> Har mahine register mein likha gaya — "theek hai". Kyunki koi ek number khatre ki line se neeche nahi tha.
> Lekin in chhe mahino mein Riya 3 centimetre lambi hui — aur **ek gram bhi wazan nahi badha.** *(She grew taller but not one gram heavier.)*
> August mein woh gambhir kuposhan mein pahunch gayi. *(In August she became severely malnourished.)*
>
> Aaj ka system har baar **sirf ek wazan** dekhta hai — jaise ek photo.
> PoshanSetu **poori film** dekhta hai — bachcha kis taraf ja raha hai.
> Usne Riya ke liye **April mein hi chetavani de di — 125 din pehle.**
>
> Chetavani ke baad kaam bhi pakka hota hai: Anganwadi didi aur Sahiya ko phone par, Hindi mein, **7 din mein ghar jaane ka kaam** milta hai. Kaam nahi hua toh **supervisor ko apne-aap pata chalta hai.**
>
> Yeh koi naya system nahi — **Poshan Tracker mein jo wazan pehle se likhe ja rahe hain, unhi se kaam karta hai.** Bina internet bhi chalta hai.
>
> 250 Anganwadi ke liye kharcha 33 lakh rupaye — ek bachche par lagbhag **330 rupaye.**
>
> **Bimari ke baad ilaaj nahi — bimari se pehle bachaav. Dhanyavaad.**

If asked: "Riya ek test ka bachcha hai, asli nahi — par aise bachche har gaon mein hain."

## 2. Easy comparisons

- **Photo vs film:** "Doctor ek photo dekhta hai. Hum poori film dekhte hain."
- **Thermometer vs weather forecast:** "Thermometer batata hai ki bukhaar hai. Hum batate hain ki bukhaar aane wala hai."
- **Report card:** "Bachcha har pariksha mein pass hai, par har mahine number gir rahe hain. Teacher ko fail hone se pehle batana chahiye."
- **Bank balance:** "Har din paisa hai, par har din ghat raha hai. Khaata khaali hone se pehle pata chalna chahiye."

## 3. Tough questions, easy answers

**1. "Doctor prescription dekh kar bata dega. Iski kya zaroorat?"**
"Doctor tab dekhta hai jab bachcha bimaar hokar uske paas jaata hai. Kuposhan dheere-dheere aata hai — bachcha bahar se theek dikhta hai, isliye maa doctor ke paas jaati hi nahi. Riya kabhi bimaar nahi dikhi. Gaon mein har bachche ko har mahine doctor nahi dekhta — Anganwadi didi dekhti hai. Hum usi har mahine ke wazan ko jodkar pehle hi bata dete hain. **Doctor ilaaj karta hai; hum bachche ko samay par doctor tak pahunchate hain.**"

**2. "Anganwadi didi khud dekh legi."**
"Ek didi ke paas 40 bachche aur kai register hain. Har bachche ke 12 mahine ke wazan milana bahut mushkil hai. Yeh kaam phone apne-aap karta hai aur sirf batata hai — kiske ghar jaana hai, kyun, aur kab tak."

**3. "Poshan Tracker toh pehle se hai."**
"Tracker wazan likhta hai. Hum batate hain kiska wazan khatre ki taraf ja raha hai aur kisko kya karna hai. Yeh Tracker ka hi agla hissa banega — alag app nahi."

**4. "Gaon mein internet nahi hai."**
"Bina internet chalta hai — network aane par bhej deta hai. Sahiya ko saadharan button wale phone par Hindi mein call aata hai — 1 dabao toh 'mil aayi'."

**5. "Didi zyada padhi-likhi nahi hai."**
"Sab Hindi mein hai aur rangon se samajh aata hai — laal: aaj aspatal; narangi: Anganwadi mein ilaaj; peela: 7 din mein ghar jaayein; neela: phir wazan karein; hara: theek hai."

**6. "Kitna paisa lagega?"**
"250 Anganwadi ke pilot mein 33 lakh — ek bachche par lagbhag 330 rupaye. Uske baad har saal 30 rupaye prati bachcha. Sarkar ke pehle se bane system use karte hain, isliye sasta hai."

**7. "Machine galat bata de toh?"**
"Har niyam WHO ke hisaab se hai, aur dono mantralaya manzoor karte hain. Har chetavani ke saath kaaran likha hota hai. Faisla didi aur doctor hi karte hain — yeh sirf yaad dilata hai."

**8. "Bachchon ki jaankari chori ho gayi toh?"**
"Swasthya ki jaankari maa ki manzoori se hi jaati hai. Upar ke afsaron ko naam nahi, sirf ginti dikhti hai. Har baar kisne dekha, yeh record hota hai."

**9. "Parivaar eent-bhatte par chala gaya toh?"**
"Bachche ko ek card milta hai jo bina internet bhi jaancha ja sakta hai. Card kho jaaye toh phone par ek code, ya maa ke angoothe se record mil jaata hai."

**10. "Kya yeh sach mein kaam karta hai?"**
"Imaandari se: abhi 1,440 bachchon ke test wale aankdon par jaancha hai — gambhir hone wale 3 mein se 2 bachche pehle pakde gaye, ausatan 66 din pehle. Agla kadam: asli aankdon par 12 mahine ka pilot."

**If you don't know:** "Yeh bahut achha sawaal hai — yahi hum pilot mein jaanchenge."

## 4. Five numbers to remember

- **9.1 kilo × 6 months:** Riya's weight didn't go up
- **125 days:** how early Riya was caught
- **2 out of 3:** children caught before becoming severe
- **66 days:** how early, on average
- **₹330:** cost per child (pilot)
